# **App Name**: GeoLink Navigator

## Core Features:

- Hero Section Display: Display a hero section with a compelling full-width image and clear call-to-action buttons.
- About Section: Showcase company history, mission, and values in a dedicated 'About' section.
- Services Section: Present surveying, mapping, GIS, digitization, and training services with concise descriptions and relevant icons.
- Projects Section: Feature a gallery to showcase completed projects with categorization for easy browsing.
- Contact Section: Embed a Google Map, contact form, and display contact details for easy communication.
- Security Measures: Enable HTTPS and implement reCAPTCHA on the contact form to enhance security.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Rich brown (#8B5E3C), symbolizing the earth and foundation of surveying.
- Background color: Soft, desaturated beige (#F5F5DC), creating a neutral, professional backdrop.
- Accent color: Forest green (#228B22), to highlight calls to action and important information with a vibrant natural touch.
- Body and headline font: 'Poppins', a modern sans-serif providing a clean, trustworthy feel. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Use clear and professional icons to represent services and project categories.
- Maintain a clean and structured layout with clear content hierarchy for ease of navigation.
- Incorporate subtle scroll-triggered animations to enhance user engagement without being distracting.
