# Variant A: Precision Atlas

## Design Stance
Editorial authority meets technical precision — like a high-end surveying journal crossed with a modern SaaS landing page. Serif headlines convey trust and longevity; coordinate labels and topographic textures signal the technical nature of the work.

## Key Choices
- **Typography:** Playfair Display (serif) for headlines + Poppins (sans) for body — editorial contrast
- **Color:** Warm cream base (#FAF6F0) with brand gold (#8B6914) and deep brown (#5C3A1E) accents
- **Texture:** Subtle topographic contour grid pattern on hero and cream sections
- **Layout:** Asymmetric hero with coordinate labels, card-based services with gold accent bars, dark brown projects section for contrast
- **Details:** Monospace coordinate readouts (LAT 7.43°N / LON 3.91°E), scroll progress bar, hover micro-animations on service cards

## Trade-offs
- **Strong at:** Authority and trust signaling, clear visual hierarchy, professional yet warm
- **Weak at:** Could feel slightly formal for a startup; serif headlines may not appeal to all tastes

## Best for
- Clients who want to feel they're working with an established, serious firm
- B2B and government contracts where credibility matters
- Positioning as a premium surveying partner rather than a commodity provider
