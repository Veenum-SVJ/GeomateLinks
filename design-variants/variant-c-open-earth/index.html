About Us

Precision Grounded
in Nigerian Soil

Geomate Links Consulting Limited has been at the forefront of geospatial innovation in Nigeria since 2007. What started as a passion for accurate surveying has grown into a full-service firm trusted by government agencies, construction companies, and land developers.

We believe that every boundary tells a story, and our job is to tell it with precision. From cadastral surveys to drone-based orthophotos, we bring the same commitment to accuracy whether the project spans acres or hectares.
