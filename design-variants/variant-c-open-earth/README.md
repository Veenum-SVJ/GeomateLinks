# Variant C: Open Earth

## Design Stance
Warm, generous, and approachable — like a premium consulting firm that puts people first. Soft rounded shapes, generous whitespace, and a friendly emoji-driven service grid. The most "startup" of the three variants.

## Key Choices
- **Typography:** DM Serif Display (elegant serif for headlines) + Outfit (friendly geometric sans for body)
- **Color:** Soft cream base, warm earth tones, gold gradients for accents
- **Layout:** Full-bleed hero with floating stat card, large rounded images, emoji service icons
- **Details:** Soft pill-shaped buttons, gradient gold accents, warm sepia map filter, generous spacing

## Trade-offs
- **Strong at:** Approachability, warmth, modern startup feel, strong visual hierarchy
- **Weak at:** May feel too casual for government/enterprise clients; emoji icons might undermine technical credibility

## Best for
- Younger clients and private sector partners
- Positions Geomate Links as modern and accessible
- Best if the brand wants to shed any "stuffy consultancy" perception
