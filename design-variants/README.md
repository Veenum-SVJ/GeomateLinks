# Geomate Links — Design Variants

Three design directions for the Geomate Links website redesign. All preserve the brand's brown/gold/green color palette and use light-dominant layouts.

## Quick Comparison

| Dimension | A: Precision Atlas | B: Field Notes | C: Open Earth |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------|---------------|
| **Tone** | Editorial, authoritative | Technical, engineering | Warm, approachable |
| **Typography** | Playfair Display (serif) | DM Sans + Space Mono | DM Serif Display + Outfit |
| **Hero** | Asymmetric with contour texture | Bold diagonal split | Full-bleed with floating card |
| **Services** | Card grid with gold accent bars | Numbered list (spec sheet) | Emoji icons, soft cards |
| **Projects** | Dark brown section | Dark section with grayscale images | Warm cream, rounded cards |
| **Best for** | B2B, government, credibility | Engineering firms, technical clients | Startup vibe, younger clients |
| **Formality** | High | Medium-high | Low-medium |

## How to View

Open each variant in your browser:

- **Windows:** `start design-variants/variant-a-precision-atlas/index.html`
- **macOS:** `open design-variants/variant-a-precision-atlas/index.html`
- **Linux:** `xdg-open design-variants/variant-a-precision-atlas/index.html`

## Recommendation

For Geomate Links' positioning as a **trusted Nigerian surveying firm** that also wants to feel **modern and approachable**, I recommend **Variant A: Precision Atlas** as the primary direction. It strikes the best balance between authority and warmth, and the topographic contour aesthetic is genuinely unique for a surveying company website.

However, **Variant C's** floating stat card and soft rounded aesthetic could be borrowed for specific sections (hero, contact) if you want more approachability.

**Variant B** is best if you want to lean heavily into the technical/engineering identity.
